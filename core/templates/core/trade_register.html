ℹ️ {% translate "How the profit is worked out" %}
  1. {% translate "Party value = invoice value on the weight the party received (shortage in transit is your loss)." %}
  2. {% translate "Sale income = party value before GST − cash discount − brokerage the party cuts − other deductions." %}
  3. {% translate "Cost = rice at landed cost (mill rate + purchase expenses) + loading + freight you carry (advance + balance the party paid the driver) + commission you pay the broker." %}
  4. {% translate "Profit = sale income − cost. Freight the party paid the driver is counted once: as your cost, and as a cut from what they pay you." %}
  5. {% translate "A truck that is not paid yet is 'awaiting payment', not a loss." %}
  6. {% translate "Profit earned = full profit × share of the money received. Half the money in, half the profit earned; fully paid, the full profit. A loss is counted in full straight away." %}
